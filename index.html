💧 ML-Based Water Quality Testing System

AI-powered tool for predictive analysis & smart treatment recommendations

How It Works

Input the measured pH, Turbidity, and Dissolved Oxygen values from your water sample. The model simulates an ML-based classification to assess water quality and suggest treatments.

Sensor Interfacing Ranges

pH Sensor: 0–14 | Ideal: 6.5–8.5

Turbidity Sensor: 0–1000 NTU | Safe: ≤5 NTU

Dissolved Oxygen: 0–20 mg/L | Ideal: ≥6 mg/L

Treatment Recommendations

✅ Good: Safe for consumption, only periodic monitoring needed.

⚠️ Moderate: Apply activated carbon + UV filtration before use.

❌ Poor: Requires chlorination, sedimentation, and advanced filtration.

Data for Good 🌍

This system contributes to clean water initiatives by providing open-source data for NGOs and water authorities to improve resource management and sustainability.
